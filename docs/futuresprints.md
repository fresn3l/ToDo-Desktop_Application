# Future sprints

Work that is specified but **not** in the current Home overhaul.

## Month / year calendar (Calendar tab)

The Calendar tab stays the **week clock** for now (lectures, dues, Fill week).

A later sprint turns that tab into a **month-long calendar, up to a year**: month grid, jump between months, year overview. The Home **Today** widget remains the day strip. The week clock may live as a month-day drill-in or a Home widget — decide when that sprint starts.

Do not start this until Home widgets have been used for a real week.

## Live Home (always-draggable widgets)

Home customization is **Edit Home** only: add, snap-drag, cycle size, remove, extra pages.

A later sprint adds **live mode**: drag and resize without entering Edit, with a clear way to avoid grabbing a to-do or journal field by mistake.

---

Older product plans (iPhone carry, Cluny proposals) stay in their own docs; they are not Home-UI sprints.
